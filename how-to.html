QUICK GUIDE

How to use
the hub

A quick guide to the main hub, all 19 worlds, profile links, graphics settings, and Shadow Hunt.

01 // THEMES

Choose your world

Open WORLDS and choose from 18 universes, including anime, superheroes, Indian cinema, and the Human Life Speedrun. Your selection is saved in this browser and applied on the main hub.

02 // CHARACTER PORTAL

Click the logo

Click the site logo to open the character screen. Close it with the button or press Escape.

03 // SOUND & EFFECTS

Customize the atmosphere

Use MUSIC for the background sound, SFX for button sounds, and SHADOWS for the floating particles. Sound only starts when you turn it on.

04 // PROFILES

Explore your links

Scroll to My Identities. Visit portal opens a profile link, and Copy username copies the name shown on the card. A few links are still being added.

05 // SHADOW HUNT

Play the mini-game

Enter a name, start the round, then click the glowing shadow orb as quickly as you can. You have 30 seconds. Your score is saved to this browser's local leaderboard.

06 // SECRET AWAKENING

Find the hidden effect

On the main hub, click the large symbol above the title to trigger a short theme-specific transformation effect. You can also focus it and press Enter or Space.

Quick tip: If the latest update doesn't appear, refresh the hub with Ctrl + F5. The Shadow Hunt leaderboard is currently local to each browser, so it isn't a shared global leaderboard.
